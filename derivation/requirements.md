### NUM-2: Stationary-geometry snap

If |v_h|^2 < 1e-13 m^2/s^2 and |s_h| <= DTHR at the start of the vertical window, relative horizontal motion is treated as zero and the horizontal condition holds for the whole window. Consequence: when |s_h| is within about sqrt(1e-13)*T (6e-5 m for T = 180 s) of DTHR and the aircraft drift apart, the reported exit time can be later than the exact one by up to the whole lookahead. Preserved as a convention for drop-in equivalence.

* Derived from: [`ACCoRD/WCV_TAUMOD.java:104-108`](https://github.com/nasa/daidalus/blob/1c7b58e5525d5cca91cbbf4a8b7337a488243769/Java/src/gov/nasa/larcfm/ACCoRD/WCV_TAUMOD.java#L104-L108), [`ACCoRD/WCV_tvar.java:158-166`](https://github.com/nasa/daidalus/blob/1c7b58e5525d5cca91cbbf4a8b7337a488243769/Java/src/gov/nasa/larcfm/ACCoRD/WCV_tvar.java#L158-L166).
* PVS: PVS uses a = 0 exactly (implementation convention that the PVS specification does not have).
* Verified by: differential testing, exact model.
* History: Refined after oracle run 1: the first version stated the snap but not its consequence; the exact oracle found 2 of 600 near-stationary vectors where DAIDALUS reported 180 s of violation against an exact exit at 6.9 s and 175 s.

### NUM-3: Closed boundaries

## Accuracy Envelope

### ACC-1: Semantic cliffs are a declared boundary band

The conflict decision is discontinuous at HMD = DTHR (the horizontal interval jumps from length TTHR to empty), at |s_h| = DTHR with zero relative horizontal velocity, and at |sz| = ZTHR in level flight. Inputs within relative margin 1e-9 of a cliff, or whose exact violation set is shorter than 1e-6 s (or misses by less than 1e-6 s), or that sit on a 1e-13 snap threshold (NUM-1/NUM-2), are boundary-band: any answer is accepted but recorded. Outside the band, answers are graded against the exact semantics with the NUM-1/NUM-2 conventions applied.

* Derived from: (derived by analysis of SEM-2/3; not stated in code or PVS).
* PVS: n/a (exact reals have no rounding) (outside the scope of the PVS specification).
* Verified by: exact model.
* History: Refined after oracle run 1: grading now applies the NUM-1/NUM-2 conventions, and the pure exact semantics is kept alongside so the convention gap is reported rather than hidden.

### ACC-2: Accuracy outside the band

Outside the boundary band, conflict and violation decisions equal the exact semantics (SEM-2..8) and interval endpoints are within 1e-6 s of the exact endpoints.
